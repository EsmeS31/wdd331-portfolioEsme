Use the controls to compare automatic rotation and a straight route. To demonstrate reduced motion, enable “Emulate CSS prefers-reduced-motion” in your browser’s DevTools.

What to notice

  • offset-path defines the route.
  • offset-distance moves the marker from 0% to 100% of that route.
  • offset-rotate: auto turns the marker to face its direction of travel.
  • When reduced motion is requested, the marker stays at its destination without animating.
